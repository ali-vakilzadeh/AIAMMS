AIAMMS05
What the copilot does

Four kinds of help, where the work happens.

Answers from your own manuals

Ask about a pump or a panel and get an answer with the document and page it came from. When the manuals don't cover it, it says so instead of guessing.

Checklists and workflows in minutes

Describe the job in a sentence, in Persian or English, and review a structured draft with measurements, safety steps and tools.

Troubleshooting when a fault is reported

Probable causes and repair steps, drawn from the manuals and from what fixed similar equipment in your own history.

Less typing on every work order

Suggested description, parts, duration and failure codes, based on similar work done before.

AIAMMS06
People stay in charge

AI you can trust, because it knows its place.

Maintenance decisions affect safety and cost. The copilot is designed to earn trust step by step, not to demand it.

  • Every output is a draft

    Nothing becomes active until an authorized person approves it.

  • It shows how sure it is, and why

    A confidence level and a short reason, such as "based on 4 similar repairs on this pump type".

  • It learns from your decisions

    Accepted, edited and rejected suggestions make the next ones better.

  • Privacy by default

    Names and identifiers are removed before anything leaves the platform. Manuals are indexed on our own servers. Organizations can switch AI off, or use their own model.

AIAMMS09
Outcome · Safety

Safety is part of the work order, not a separate system.

RUN

Inspect while running

The task can be done with the equipment in operation.

PAUSE

Stop briefly

The equipment must pause for the task, then may resume.

STOP

Stop until complete

The equipment, and what it belongs to, is shown as stopped until the work is done.

Permits such as lockout or hot work must be recorded before a step can start.

Only authorized people can clear a stop, always with a reason.

Downtime is recorded automatically when equipment stops and restarts.

Outcome

Everyone sees, on the work order and on a QR scan, when equipment must not run.
